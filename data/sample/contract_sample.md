# Supply Agreement CT-0002

Between the Buyer and Siam Supply Co., Ltd. (the "Supplier"), vendor V-0002.

## 1. Definitions

"Goods" means the materials and parts listed in Schedule 1. "Order" means a purchase order issued by the Buyer under this Agreement.

## 2. Scope of Supply

The Supplier shall supply the Goods ordered by the Buyer from time to time. This Agreement does not oblige the Buyer to order any minimum quantity.

## 3. Price and Price Adjustment

Every 6 months the parties review prices. A change of more than 10% in the reference commodity index entitles either party to a proportionate adjustment.

## 4. Payment Terms

The Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days of the invoice date (net 45). A discount of 1% applies to any invoice settled within 10 days.

## 5. Volume Rebate

Annual volume rebate: 2% once annual spend passes USD 1,455,000. The rebate is not paid automatically: the Buyer must claim it in writing after the end of the calendar year.

## 6. Delivery and Late Delivery

The Supplier shall deliver on the date stated in each Order. Late deliveries attract liquidated damages of 1.5% of the delayed order value per week, up to a maximum of 10%.

## 7. Quality and Inspection

Goods must conform to the agreed specification. The Buyer may inspect and reject non-conforming Goods within 14 days of receipt.

## 8. Warranty

The Supplier warrants the goods against defects in material and workmanship for 24 months from delivery.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's commercial and technical information confidential for five years after termination.

## 10. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party promptly.

## 11. Term and Termination

This Agreement runs for three years from its effective date. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 60 days' written notice.

## 12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Thailand. Disputes are referred to arbitration in Bangkok.
